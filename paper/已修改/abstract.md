# Abstract

Cross-sectional stock-return prediction with neural networks commonly optimises Mean Squared Error (MSE) or a close relative, yet the downstream decision — a long-short portfolio — depends on the cross-sectional rank of the predictions, not on their calibrated magnitudes. Under this mismatch, a quadratic loss gives disproportionate training weight to heavy-tailed outliers, and the resulting model trades ranking quality for calibration quality that the portfolio does not use. This report asks whether a hybrid loss that combines a directional-accuracy term with a robust magnitude term can outperform both traditional regression losses and pure directional losses under a controlled evaluation protocol.

The study uses a CRSP-style US equity monthly panel with a static protocol: training window `1990-01` to `1994-12` (60 monthly cross-sections) and main out-of-sample test window `1995-01` to `1996-12` (24 monthly cross-sections). The model is a fixed multi-layer perceptron (MLP) with hidden widths `[64, 32, 16]`, ReLU activations, and dropout $0.2$, consuming 15 model-input columns (see Chapter 4 §4.4.1 and Table 4.3). Training uses batch size $1024$ and 20 epochs of Adam; the trained model is then evaluated month-by-month on a top/bottom-10% signal-tilted long-short portfolio with a 5% per-name cap. Performance is reported as annualised Sharpe ($\sqrt{12}$-scaled monthly mean/std), cumulative 24-month return, and, for multi-seed phases, the coefficient of variation $\mathrm{CV} = \sigma_S / |\mu_S|$ across three random seeds.

Four comparison phases plus one normalisation probe are run. Within each comparison table, the loss function is the only varying factor; cross-phase comparisons differ in additional dimensions (§3.8). A single-seed baseline compares seven loss families (MSE, MedSE, MADL, GMADL, IMADL, and two hybrid multiplicative variants). A Phase 1.5 sweep scans nine parameterised hybrid variants (five additive A-series and four multiplicative M-series). Phase 2 refines the M2-robust loss with a γ parameter and evaluates five γ settings across three seeds. An integrated Phase 2 summary extends the comparison to IMADL-m2 α, IMADL-GMADL β, and adaptive-λ sweeps under the same multi-seed protocol. Finally, a Phase 2.2-fix1 normalisation probe tests loss-component normalisation on the top three candidates as a diagnostic follow-up.

The multi-seed evidence identifies `m2_robust_gamma07` (multiplicative hybrid with robustness parameter $\gamma = 0.07$) as the best-supported choice. It achieves mean Sharpe $0.9156$, CV $0.1808$, and mean cumulative return $+27.99\%$ across three seeds; no seed produces a negative Sharpe in this configuration, and the result is approximately flat under loss-component normalisation (normalised mean Sharpe $0.9112$). The high-return alternative `m2_robust_gamma10` reaches a mean Sharpe of $1.0043$ but has CV $0.5613$ — three times that of γ07 — and degrades to mean Sharpe $0.4072$ under normalisation; it is only appropriate when higher seed sensitivity is acceptable. The stable fallback `imadl_m2_alpha06`, drawn from an independent parameterisation, achieves mean Sharpe $0.6895$ with CV $0.2443$ and the largest mean cumulative return of the three candidates at $+30.42\%$; it is selected by the stability/return trade-off rather than by Sharpe dominance. Traditional regression losses (MSE, MedSE) and pure directional losses (MADL, GMADL, IMADL) do not produce economically meaningful positive Sharpes in the seed-42 same-window baseline, and the absolute-loss family additionally exhibits very negative point-prediction R² values while retaining competitive ranking performance — a dissociation between calibration and portfolio quality that the report makes explicit.

Three boundaries constrain the conclusions. First, the evaluation uses a single static 24-month window; the rolling-window extension is listed as future work. Second, multi-seed evaluations use three seeds, which is sufficient to differentiate CV at the order-of-magnitude level but not to bound CV to the second decimal. Third, the loss-component normalisation probe's scale ratios are diagnostics-estimated rather than measured by a per-component logger, which is flagged as a priority instrumentation task. Within these boundaries the report provides a verifiable recommendation — `m2_robust_gamma07` as the primary loss, with explicit high-return and stable-fallback alternatives — together with a source-of-truth evidence map that ties every reported number to a named artifact file.

**Keywords.** loss function design; robust regression; MADL; GMADL; cross-sectional stock-return prediction; long-short portfolio; multi-layer perceptron; multi-seed robustness; coefficient of variation; CRSP.

# 摘要

基于神经网络的横截面股票收益预测通常以均方误差（MSE）或其近似变体作为优化目标，然而下游决策——多空组合——仅依赖预测值的横截面排名而非其校准幅度。在此错配下，二次损失函数对重尾异常值赋予过高的训练权重，导致模型牺牲组合所需的排序质量，换取组合并不直接使用的校准质量。本报告研究一种将方向准确性项与稳健幅度项相结合的混合损失函数，能否在受控评估协议下同时优于传统回归损失和纯方向性损失。

本研究使用CRSP风格的美国股票月度面板数据，采用静态协议：训练窗口为1990年1月至1994年12月（60个月度横截面），主样本外测试窗口为1995年1月至1996年12月（24个月度横截面）。模型为固定的多层感知机（MLP），隐藏层宽度为[64, 32, 16]，ReLU激活函数，dropout为0.2，输入15列模型特征（见第4章§4.4.1及表4.3）。训练使用批量大小1024和20轮Adam优化器；训练后的模型逐月在前/后10%信号加权多空组合（单名上限5%）上进行评估。绩效指标包括年化夏普比率（$\sqrt{12}$缩放的月度均值/标准差）、24个月累计收益，以及多种子阶段的变异系数$\mathrm{CV} = \sigma_S / |\mu_S|$（三个随机种子）。

实验包含四个比较阶段和一个归一化探测。在每个比较表内，损失函数是唯一变化因素；跨阶段比较在额外维度上有所不同（§3.8）。单种子基线比较七个损失族（MSE、MedSE、MADL、GMADL、IMADL及两个混合乘法变体）。Phase 1.5扫描九个参数化混合变体（五个加法A系列和四个乘法M系列）。Phase 2以γ参数细化M2-robust损失，在三个种子上评估五个γ设置。集成Phase 2摘要将比较扩展至IMADL-m2 α、IMADL-GMADL β和自适应λ扫描。最后，Phase 2.2-fix1诊断性归一化探测作为诊断性后续测试损失分量归一化对前三名候选的影响。

多种子证据表明`m2_robust_gamma07`（稳健参数$\gamma = 0.07$的乘法混合损失）为最佳支持选择。其均值夏普比率为0.9156，CV为0.1808，均值累计收益为+27.99%（三个种子）；无种子产生负夏普比率，且在损失分量归一化下近似稳定（归一化均值夏普0.9112）。高收益备选`m2_robust_gamma10`均值夏普为1.0043但CV为0.5613——为γ07的三倍——归一化后降至0.4072；仅在可接受较高种子敏感性时适用。稳定后备`imadl_m2_alpha06`来自独立参数化，均值夏普0.6895，CV为0.2443，三候选中累计收益最高（+30.42%）；其选择基于稳定性/收益权衡而非夏普主导。传统回归损失（MSE、MedSE）和纯方向性损失（MADL、GMADL、IMADL）在种子42同窗口基线中未产生经济意义上的正夏普比率。

三项边界约束结论。第一，评估使用单一静态24个月窗口；滚动窗口扩展列为未来工作。第二，多种子评估使用三个种子，足以在数量级水平区分CV但不足以将CV精确到小数点后第二位。第三，归一化探测的尺度比率为诊断估计值而非由逐分量记录器测量，这被标记为优先仪器化任务。在这些边界内，本报告提供可验证的推荐——`m2_robust_gamma07`作为主要损失函数，并附有明确的高收益和稳定后备替代方案——以及将每个报告数字追溯至命名工件文件的证据溯源图。
