\chapter*{Abstract}
\section*{}
Cross-sectional stock-return prediction with neural networks commonly optimises Mean Squared Error (MSE), yet the downstream decision --- a long-short portfolio --- depends on the cross-sectional rank of predictions rather than their calibrated magnitudes. This mismatch causes quadratic losses to waste capacity on heavy-tailed outliers that the portfolio ignores. Directional penalties such as the Mean Absolute Directional Loss (MADL) address ranking directly but discard magnitude information, limiting gradient signal when predictions are correctly signed but poorly scaled. This report investigates whether a hybrid loss combining a directional-accuracy term with a robust magnitude term can outperform both traditional regression losses and pure directional losses.

The study uses a CRSP-style US equity monthly panel with a static train/test protocol (train \texttt{1990-01..1994-12}, test \texttt{1995-01..1996-12}, 24 months out-of-sample). The model is a multi-layer perceptron (MLP $[64, 32, 16]$, ReLU, dropout $0.2$) consuming 15 input features. Performance is evaluated on a top/bottom-10\% signal-tilted long-short portfolio with a 5\% per-name cap, reported as annualised Sharpe, cumulative return, and cross-seed coefficient of variation (CV) across three random seeds.

Through a systematic series of experiments --- baseline loss comparison, parameterised hybrid sweeps, multi-seed robustness evaluation, and a loss-component normalisation probe --- the study identifies \texttt{m2\_robust\_gamma07} (multiplicative hybrid, $\gamma = 0.07$) as the best-supported choice. It achieves mean Sharpe $0.9156$, CV $0.1808$, and mean cumulative return $+27.99\%$ across three seeds, with no seed producing a negative Sharpe. The result is approximately stable under component normalisation (normalised mean Sharpe $0.9112$). Traditional regression losses and pure directional losses do not produce economically meaningful positive Sharpes under the same protocol.

Three boundaries constrain the conclusions: the evaluation uses a single static 24-month window, multi-seed evaluations use three seeds (sufficient for order-of-magnitude CV differentiation but not second-decimal precision), and the normalisation probe's scale ratios are diagnostics-estimated. Within these boundaries the report provides a verifiable recommendation with explicit high-return (\texttt{m2\_robust\_gamma10}) and stable-fallback (\texttt{imadl\_m2\_alpha06}) alternatives.

\noindent\textbf{摘要：}使用神经网络进行横截面股票收益预测时，通常优化均方误差（MSE），但下游决策——多空组合——依赖于预测的横截面排序而非其校准幅度。这种错配导致二次损失将容量浪费在组合忽略的厚尾异常值上。方向性惩罚如平均绝对方向损失（MADL）直接针对排序，但丢弃幅度信息，当预测方向正确但尺度不佳时梯度信号受限。本文研究一种结合方向准确性项与稳健幅度项的混合损失是否能优于传统回归损失和纯方向性损失。

研究使用CRSP风格的美国股票月度面板数据，采用静态训练/测试协议（训练期1990-01至1994-12，测试期1995-01至1996-12，24个月样本外）。模型为多层感知机（MLP $[64, 32, 16]$，ReLU，dropout $0.2$），输入15个特征。绩效通过前/后10\%信号倾斜多空组合（单名上限5\%）评估，报告年化Sharpe比率、累计收益率及三个随机种子的变异系数（CV）。

通过一系列系统实验——基线损失对比、参数化混合扫描、多种子稳健性评估及损失分量归一化探针——研究确定\texttt{m2\_robust\_gamma07}（乘法混合，$\gamma = 0.07$）为最优选择。该配置在三个种子上实现平均Sharpe $0.9156$、CV $0.1808$、平均累计收益$+27.99\%$，且无种子产生负Sharpe。传统回归损失和纯方向性损失在相同协议下未能产生经济意义上的正Sharpe。
